Trust is harder than it looks in modern image workflows.

Images online are routinely edited, re-exported, compressed, screenshotted, or stripped of original source clues. A picture can look convincing while carrying traces of heavy post-processing — or lose innocent context through ordinary platform handling.

Editing is frictionless

Simple retouching tools can alter context quickly, often without obvious visual artifacts at first glance.

Distribution mutates files

Resharing across apps can remove metadata, resize images, and alter compression patterns unexpectedly.

Visual intuition isn’t enough

Trust decisions need structured clues, not just instinct or overconfident AI-style claims.

FAQ

Is PixelProof an AI detector?

No. PixelProof is an authenticity clue-checking tool. It evaluates practical file and quality signals instead of claiming to detect AI-generated content with absolute certainty.

Can missing metadata prove an image is fake?

Not by itself. Metadata can be removed for normal reasons, including social media re-encoding. Missing metadata is one signal, not definitive proof.

Does PixelProof store uploaded images?

In this demo, files stay in your local browser session. Production implementations can be configured with strict retention and privacy controls.

Can PixelProof verify every image?

No single tool can guarantee verification for every case. PixelProof helps surface clues and confidence boundaries so decisions are better informed.
